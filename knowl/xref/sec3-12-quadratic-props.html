Properties of Quadratic Functions.

Recall that a quadratic function takes the form \(y = ax^2 + bx + c\)
🔗
  • If \(a \gt 0\text{,}\) the parabola opens upward, and the vertex is the absolute minimum of the function.
    🔗
    🔗
  • If \(a \lt 0\text{,}\) the parabola opens downward, and the vertex is the absolute maximum of the function.
    🔗
    🔗
  • The vertex is located at the point
    \begin{gather*} \lrpar{-\dfrac{b}{2a},f\lrpar{-\dfrac{b}{2a}}} \end{gather*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • Quadratics can have two, one, or zero real roots
